# Text Doorman Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 6, 2026 · Developer: Text Doorman · Contact: admin@textdoorman.com_

Text Doorman silences spam text messages from numbers that aren't in your contacts. It was designed so that your messages never leave your phone.

## What the app accesses

With your permission (Android "notification access"), Text Doorman reads the notifications posted by your texting app, such as Google Messages or Samsung Messages. From a text notification it uses:

- the sender's name or number as shown in the notification, and
- the message text.

It ignores notifications from all other apps. It does not request access to your contacts, call history, location, or the messages stored in your texting app.

## How it's used

Each text from a number that isn't a saved contact is checked by a spam model that runs entirely on your phone. If the text looks like spam, the app hides its notification. The text itself stays in your texting app.

## What's stored, and where

On your phone only, in the app's private storage:

- **Screening history:** up to the last 500 texts from unknown numbers, with whether each was silenced. You can clear it in Settings → Clear history.
- **Your corrections:** texts you mark "Spam" or "Not spam" and their sender numbers, used to personalize screening. You can delete them in Settings → Forget my corrections.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. The app excludes this data from Android cloud backup.

## What's shared

Nothing automatically. Text Doorman has no servers and does not collect, upload, sell, or share any data. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries.

If you choose to use **Settings → Send feedback**, the app shows you a summary first (counts of screened texts, your phone model and Android version, your answers, and only the texts you select, with sender numbers removed and long numbers masked). It is sent only if you tap Send and choose an app such as email.

[If in-app purchases are added: Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Text Doorman receives only confirmation that a purchase was made.]

## Children

Text Doorman is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
